Teaching & Mentoring

My teaching is grounded in technically rigorous, inclusive computing education connected to real-world problems. I help students develop technical competence while thinking critically about the social consequences of data, cloud computing, AI, and information systems.

I teach undergraduate and graduate courses in HCI, business intelligence, databases, cloud infrastructure, and enterprise systems. Course development includes hands-on AWS and generative AI activities, authentic healthcare datasets, and shorter, searchable, captioned video modules that students can revisit at their own pace.
