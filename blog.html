Research Blog

This is a space for my thinking, reflections, and ideas as a researcher and educator. I write about questions that emerge while reading papers, designing studies, working with communities, and teaching—especially where human-centered AI meets everyday life.

About this notebook

My research asks how people understand and collaborate with intelligent systems. Here, I want to explore the reasoning behind that work: what makes an explanation useful, how we build trust without overlooking uncertainty, and how the people affected by a technology can help shape its design.

Some posts will reflect on a paper or project; others will share methodological lessons, observations from teaching and mentoring, or ideas that are still taking shape. I see this notebook as a place to think in public, revisit assumptions, and connect questions across healthcare, recommender systems, and community collaboration.

You will find working notes alongside more developed reflections. They capture my perspective at the time of writing, and may evolve as I learn from new evidence, conversations, and experience.
