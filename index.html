About

I am a tenure-track Assistant Professor in the Department of Information Systems and Quantitative Analysis (ISQA) at the University of Nebraska at Omaha, where I have been a faculty member since 2021. I also hold a courtesy Assistant Professor appointment in the Division of Health System Sciences, Department of Pediatrics, at the University of Nebraska Medical Center (2026–present), and an Affiliate Graduate Faculty appointment in the Department of Public Health and Health Sciences at the University of South Dakota (2026–2031).

My research focuses on human-centered AI at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction, explainable AI, generative AI, healthcare, and social computing. I study how people understand, use, and collaborate with intelligent systems, with an emphasis on transparency, user control, and accountable system design.

Before joining UNO, I was an Assistant Research Professor at Penn State University (2019–2021), working with John M. Carroll. I earned my Ph.D. in Information Science at the University of Pittsburgh in 2019, advised by Peter Brusilovsky.
