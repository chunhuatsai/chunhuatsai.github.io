Short Bio

I am an Assistant Professor in Information Systems and Quantitative Analysis at the University of Nebraska at Omaha and a courtesy Assistant Professor at the University of Nebraska Medical Center. My research connects Human-Computer Interaction and AI to design conversational, explainable, and accountable systems for healthcare and communities. Before joining UNO in 2021, I was an Assistant Research Professor at Penn State. I earned my Ph.D. in Information Science from the University of Pittsburgh in 2019.

My work has been supported by NSF CAREER and CRII awards as Principal Investigator and an NSF DASS award as Co-Investigator. I have received Best Paper Awards at ACM Learning @ Scale (2022) and the International Conference on Communities and Technologies (2021), and was selected as a 2025–2026 NSF NAIRR AI Education Fellow.
